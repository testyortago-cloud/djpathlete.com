Pipeline board editor and hand-made cards — review sheet

G29 · /admin/pipeline and /admin/pipeline/settings · captured 2026-09-22 from the real app on the dev clone, tenant Primary. Twelve shots, numbered in the order a coach meets them. Callouts are burned into each PNG — open any file on its own and it explains itself. Every image is 2880px wide (1440 CSS px at deviceScaleFactor 2); nothing is scaled up.

What was written, and what was not. Shots 00–10 change nothing: the stage editor's Save, rename and Create board are never pressed, and the two buttons that really do send something (shot 05's archive, shot 10's add) are both aimed at a request the server refuses before it writes — proved afterwards by re-reading the board. Shot 11 is different, and deliberately so: four real cards were created on the dev clone by clicking through the dialog, because a fixture proves render, not origination. They were not deleted afterwards. Full list in What was created at the foot of this page.
