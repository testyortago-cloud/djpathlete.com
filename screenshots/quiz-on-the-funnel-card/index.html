The quiz lives in the funnel — what was verified

Every frame is the real app on its real route, driven by scripts/capture-quiz-on-the-funnel-card.ts. The callouts are burned into each PNG.

Measured on the dev copy (anjvztjiokcgiyhobknq), asserted in the DOM before each shot:

  • No quizzes screen, and no link to one. a[href="/admin/funnels/quizzes"] count is zero on the funnels board.
  • The retired URL forwards. Navigating to /admin/funnels/quizzes ends on /admin/funnels — a bookmark gets the screen that replaced it, not a 404.
  • The quiz is on its funnel's card, located by href and then clicked. It reaches the question editor; no 404.
  • A funnel with no quiz never says the word. The second card's entire textContent is scanned against /quiz/i and must not match. This is the white-label requirement as an assertion — and an absence assertion needs a presence control, which is why both cards are in the same frame.
  • The quiz funnel was made through the real New funnel dialog — template Run a quiz — and deleted again at the end of the run.

One real hole, deliberately left open and not fixed here.

deleteFunnel deletes only the funnel row — it leaves the quiz behind. With no list screen, such a quiz is reachable only by typing its URL. It is not reachable forwards: a quiz cannot be created without a funnel, because POST /api/admin/funnels with the quiz template creates the pair in one call and deletes the quiz if the funnel insert fails. So the leak needs someone to delete a quiz funnel first, and production currently has no quizzes at all.

Closing it means deleting quizzes when their last funnel goes — destructive, and it needs an explicit decision, not a quiet one. The old list screen signposted this hole; it never fixed it, and it cost every customer a permanent top-level concept in exchange.
