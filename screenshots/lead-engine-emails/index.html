New Lead Nurture — the three emails

What a lead receives after submitting a funnel form, now live in production. Each capture's header strip shows the real send metadata; the body below it is the exact rendered email. The layout follows the house email brand from lib/email.ts: the dark Green Azure header band under the Gray Orange accent strip, the letter-spaced Lexend Exa wordmark, Lexend Deca body copy, and the warm footer treatment.

How these were produced: the live sequence_steps and business_settings rows were read from the production database and passed through renderSequenceEmail — the same function the tick runner sends with. The step-*.html / step-*.txt files beside this page are the exact HTML and plain-text parts byte-for-byte; nothing was mocked or re-typed. The lead is a sample ("Alex"), and the unsubscribe link carries a visibly fake PREVIEW-TOKEN so this preview cannot unsubscribe anyone.
