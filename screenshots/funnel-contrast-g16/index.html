Funnel contrast pass — what changed, and how to check it

Every image below is the real product: the actual funnel page on its real route (/preview/<slug>), driven in a browser on the dev clone. Nothing is a mockup or a component harness. The numbered callouts are burned into the PNGs, so each file explains itself if you open it on its own.

Read the two pages as one pair, not two examples. Neither page can show the whole fix. The dark page is the only one where the body-copy defect existed at all — on a light palette the old token already scored 5.98:1 and was fine. The light page is where the pricing card's missing boundary reads most clearly. A single "after" screenshot would have proved half the work.

3. A quiz card on a coloured band — the worst one

This is the clearest of the three, and the only one where the page was genuinely broken rather than merely hard to read. The quiz card always paints itself white, whatever colour the band behind it is. But its words were taking their colour from the band, not from the card — so on a colour scheme whose band pairs with white text, the card's writing was white on white.

Why nobody had hit this yet. Every page in production today is on the app's default colours, and on those the mismatch happens to be harmless — the wrong colour is a mid-brown that reads perfectly well on a white card (measured 9.35:1). The pairing was still wrong; it just was not visible. It turns into the picture on the left the first time a coach picks a colour scheme, because the scheme then makes that colour pure white or pure black. This page had to be put onto a scheme deliberately to photograph it, and was put back afterwards.

What is still wrong, in the "after" shots

The button's FILL is still dark-on-dark on the ink preset — look at marker 2. The ring makes its boundary findable, which is the accessibility requirement, but the button still does not look like the thing you are meant to press. That is not this change failing; it is a separate defect one level upstream, logged as G20. When a coach does not supply an accent colour, the palette invents one by rotating the brand's hue — and hue rotation keeps the same brightness. The ink brand sits at lightness 0.009 and its invented accent at 0.010, so the two are the same darkness and neither can stand out against the other. Three of the twelve presets have this problem. Fixing it means changing the colour the button is painted, which is a different decision from adding an edge, so it was deliberately not folded into this pass.
