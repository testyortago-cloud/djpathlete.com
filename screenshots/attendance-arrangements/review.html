Attendance arrangements

Clients coached in person whom a partner facility bills through its own system. Captured against the real admin app on the dev clone, 29 Aug 2026.

What these shots prove

A client with no pack can be checked in. Every arrangement below was created through the real dialog on the real client page, and every check-in is a real click on the real button. Nothing was seeded.

The check-in burned no credit. The session_checkins row is read back out of the database by the capture script and asserted field by field: credit_delta = 0, client_package_id = null, method = coach_tap. The script fails if any of those is wrong.

Nothing reached the books. An arrangement has no price and never touches client_packages, so the bookkeeping income adapter, the renewal-reminder cron and Stripe never see it.

Decisions worth knowing about

  • Coach tap only. The QR roster and the personal check-in link still refuse an arrangement client. This ledger is your evidence of work done at someone else's facility, so you record it — a client forgetting to tap would quietly undercount you. One flag flips it later.
  • A pack always wins. If a client has pack credits, those burn first and attendance is not recorded. Starting an arrangement for someone holding a pack warns you about exactly this.
  • No facility entity. “Billed by” is free text. A facilities table is speculative until there is a second one.
  • Ending an arrangement keeps its history. Sessions already recorded stay on the client and still count on the month they happened in — including a month the arrangement ended part-way through.

Re-running this

PORT=3050 npm run dev, then APP=http://localhost:3050 npx tsx scripts/capture-attendance-arrangements.ts .env.local. Add --clean to remove the arrangements and check-ins it created.
