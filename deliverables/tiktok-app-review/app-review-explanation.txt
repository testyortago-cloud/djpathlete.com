DarrenJPaul is a strength-coaching website. Coaches post their own training videos to their own TikTok account.

Login Kit (user.info.basic): when a coach links TikTok on our Connections page, we read the display name and username only, to show which account is linked.

video.publish (Direct Post): in Content Studio the coach clicks Publish now or Schedule, which opens our Post to TikTok screen. It loads creator info and shows the account name, a video preview and the editable caption. The coach must choose who can view the video (no default), can turn on comments, duets and stitches (all off by default), can disclose commercial content, and sees TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation. Nothing posts until the coach confirms.

video.upload: on the same screen, Send to TikTok drafts uploads the video to the coach's TikTok inbox, where they finish and post it in the TikTok app.

Coaches can unlink TikTok at any time on the Connections page.
